# Top 10 Prioritized Design Changes

| Priority | Change / decision | Expected benefit | Impact / trade-off | Sequence |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm site, municipality, survey and governing statutory/fire/access basis | Prevents designing the wrong orientation, discharge, parking, drainage or code solution | May force footprint/entrance changes | First |
| 2 | Commission code-based evacuation and accessible-emergency-egress study | Resolves crowd concentration, travel, door sets, refuges and final discharge | May add exits/vomitories/ramps or narrow the bowl | Before concept freeze |
| 3 | Establish geotechnical/environmental load basis and analyse roof/lateral/foundations | Converts structural imagery into a credible system and cost basis | Member/grid/foundation sizes may change | Parallel with 2 |
| 4 | Redesign/test horizontal sightlines and IMAG/screens | Improves worship visibility for side/rear seats | May reduce capacity or change block/stage geometry | Before floor/roof freeze |
| 5 | Resolve rake transitions, choir access, doors and accessible WCs | Reduces falls/exclusion and makes clear widths real | More detail/space; possible seat loss | Before engineering |
| 6 | Develop coordinated ventilation, acoustic, AV, fire and electrical concepts | Secures ceiling/roof/plant/shaft/load allowances before they become expensive | Adds capital/energy/maintenance obligations | Before roof/interior freeze |
| 7 | Design roof drainage and high-level access | Reduces leakage, ponding and unsafe maintenance | Walkways/access may affect structure and appearance | With structural concept |
| 8 | Create a legible, shaded main entrance and site arrival sequence | Handles large crowds and gives the church an appropriate civic identity | Forecourt/canopy/site cost | After site confirmation |
| 9 | Run fabricator/erector/QS option study | Rationalises 50 m trusses, 9.5 m purlin bay, splices, cranes and local sourcing | Requires early specialist fees; may change grid | Before cost-plan freeze |
| 10 | Reissue BIM package with current viewing copy and connectivity checks | Prevents stale-model review and catches load-path gaps | Minor workflow effort | Immediately / next issue |

## Sequencing note

Priorities 1–4 are decision gates. Do not spend heavily on façade or detailed MEP layouts until the site, evacuation, structural basis and bowl geometry are stable. Conversely, do not fix the roof geometry without reserving acoustic, AV, ventilation, drainage and maintenance requirements.
