# Review Limitations

This is an independent concept-stage model review, not a certificate or statutory determination.

## Information not available

- Municipality, site address, adopted/amended building by-laws and authority interpretations.
- Boundary/topographic/utilities survey, true north, adjacent risks, roads, parking, fire access and assembly areas.
- Geotechnical investigation, groundwater and foundation design criteria.
- Structural loads, wind/rain/seismic basis, material grades, analysis, member/connection/foundation calculations.
- Fire strategy, smoke/control/suppression design, door hardware and evacuation management.
- Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, sanitation, drainage, fire-water, acoustic and AV designs.
- Cost plan, procurement strategy, local fabricator quotations and construction programme.

## Method limits

- Model auditing was read-only. No geometry was repaired or regenerated.
- Existing render/drawing outputs were used only where their embedded revision was PL01; the standalone viewing copy was rejected as stale.
- The source route graph and C-value recurrence were inspected and challenged, but no full pedestrian simulation, fire dynamics model, structural finite-element analysis, acoustic model or thermal simulation was performed.
- Horizontal sightline angles are geometric diagnostics to the modelled pulpit; they are not legal pass/fail criteria.
- Absence from the model is reported as **not represented**, not proof that a consultant has done no off-model work.
- The secondary online transcription of the 1977 Model Building By-laws was used only to identify the likely code family. The official text, municipal adoption and amendments must be obtained from the applicable authority.

## Areas that could not be assessed

Actual structural safety; foundation capacity; fire-code compliance; evacuation time; smoke tenability; sanitary fixture adequacy; fresh-air rates and temperature; reverberation/intelligibility; electrical capacity/resilience; drainage capacity; site fit; parking/traffic; planning permission; accessibility compliance; cost; local material availability; and construction programme.
