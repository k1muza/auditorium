# Executive Review for the Church Building Committee

## Bottom line

The concept is organised and measurable, but it should **not be treated as construction-ready or submitted as a settled design**. The model genuinely contains 3,000 congregation seats, a large platform, supporting rooms, ten hall exit openings, a step-free route to the stage, and a repeated long-span frame. The source files are also unusually well named and reproducible.

The next spending decision should be a short concept-validation phase, not detailed drawings.

## Five decisions that matter most

1. **Test evacuation properly.** The model's shortest-route sensitivity sends roughly 1,000 people to each of the two cross-aisle exits, and the current results use placeholder factors rather than confirmed Zimbabwean/local requirements.
2. **Resolve wheelchair evacuation.** Eight wheelchair places at the rear upper level rely on a lift and unprotected refuge allowances; sixteen mid-level places reach adjacent outside stairs rather than a direct ramp.
3. **Engineer the roof and foundations.** The nine 50 m trusses, purlins, bracing, connections and pads are coordination geometry, not calculations or designed members.
4. **Improve side-seat viewing.** Independent measurement found 542 seats more than 45° off-axis to the pulpit and 184 more than 60°; screens are assumed but not modelled.
5. **Design the missing building systems.** Ventilation, acoustics, fire systems, toilets, roof drainage, resilient power and maintenance access are mostly empty allowances.

## What can be retained

- Exactly 3,000 congregation chairs are present and separated from 30 wheelchair, 30 companion and 144 choir places.
- Columns remain outside the seating bowl; all 68 columns have aligned pad-footing reference geometry.
- A front accessible entry concept, ramps, lift and 1:12 platform ramp are represented.
- The repeated structural grid, disciplined naming and reproducible model are a good base for further work.

## Recommended next commission

Appoint a local architect, fire engineer, access consultant, structural/geotechnical team, MEP engineers, acoustician/AV consultant and quantity surveyor for a coordinated option study. Confirm the site and municipality first. Then compare at least two bowl/egress/sightline options before committing to the 50 m-wide single-level solution.
